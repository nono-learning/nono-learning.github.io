افتح التطبيق ← «ملفي» ← «ركن الأهل» ← «امسح حسابي» (أو «امسح كل البيانات» في النسخة الأولى)، وحل المسألة. الحساب وكل البيانات بيتمسحوا على طول.

سياسة الخصوصية

Delete your Nono & Learning account

You can delete your account and all your children's data in one of two ways:

1. In the app (fastest)

Open the app → “My profile” → “Parents' corner” → “Delete my account” (or “Delete all data” in the first version), then solve the sum. The account and all its data are deleted right away.

2. Without the app

Send us the email you signed in with, by email or on the Nono Facebook page. We will delete the account within one week at the latest and reply to you.

Send a deletion request by email

Or write to: nonolearning2026@gmail.com

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account.
  • Your children's names, ages, favourite colours and all their progress.

We keep nothing after deletion. Drawings are only on the phone and are removed when the app is uninstalled.
